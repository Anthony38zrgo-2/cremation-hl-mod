# House of the Dead 2 civilian voice sources

Extracted from the supplied DATA.CAB and converted from stereo PCM to 22050 Hz mono PCM for GoldSrc. The archive names identify the selected civilian voice variants.

| Role | Male source | Female source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Help | F14165_66_Y_M.wav | F14126_202_1_Y_WA.wav |
| Fear | F14143_215_Y_M.wav | F14133_204_Y_W.wav |
| Thanks | F14151_421_M_YNG.wav | F14154_422_W_YNG.wav |
| Pain | F14144_220_Y_M.wav | F14122_190_Y_W.wav |

The fear clips are different lines: the man says "Don't kill me" and the woman says "No, no, no". The names do not establish an exact "I don't want to die" recording for both genders.
